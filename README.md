Vice City Cover Studio — an offline web app that turns any photo and a caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art: six poster layouts, six Vice City sunset and neon colour grades, five one-click looks, face-aware crops that keep the subject's face in the hero close-up, bold condensed lettering with a 3D extrusion, and one-click PNG export in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080. Photos never leave the machine; everything runs from static files with no network request.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — add your photos, pick a layout and grade, edit the caption, drag each photo into place, and export the PNG.

## What you get

- **6 poster layouts** — Hero Left, Hero + 3, Four Grid, Full Bleed, Triple Strip, Duo.
- **6 colour grades** — Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Miami Pop, Golden Hour, Palm Noir, Ultra Violet — with a strength slider.
- **5 one-click looks** — Sunset Heist, Neon Nights, Miami Pop, Gold Rush, Noir Drive.
- **Face-aware crops** — the subject's skin-tone focal point is measured when a photo loads, so the hero close-up keeps the face in frame; drag or zoom any panel to adjust.
- **Caption lettering** — big condensed capitals with a 3D extrusion and a neon script subtitle, in white, sunset-gradient, neon-pink or hollow-outline styles, top or bottom, with a size slider.
- **3 export sizes** — 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, 1920 × 1080 PNG, one at a time or all three at once, plus a synthwave sun, palm silhouettes, film grain and vignette.
